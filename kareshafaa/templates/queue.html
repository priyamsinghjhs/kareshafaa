Patient appointment requests

How requests work

Sent from the patient app. Nothing is booked until you confirm. Answer-by times: T0 2 h, T1 8 h, T2 24 h, T3 and screening 48 h. If you decline, the patient is told the reason in their language.

Patient password reset requests

Before you send a code

From "Forgot password?" on the sign-in page, after the patient's date of birth, ID ending and mobile matched. Check who the patient is (in person with photo ID, or a call to the registered mobile), then send a one-time reset code from the patient record.
