# Usability test plan (English and Arabic)

Status: **plan, not yet run.** No participants have been recruited. Results go in section 7 when it is run.

## 1. Questions

1. Can a coordinator find and act on the most urgent work (critical read-back needed, overdue, result needs
   action) without help?
2. Can a clinician record a critical-result read-back and triage within 2 minutes?
3. Can a patient, in **English** and in **Arabic** (right-to-left), understand their result message, say what to do
   next and by when (teach-back), confirm or ask to change an appointment, and stop contact?
4. Do staff understand the wording "result needs action", "owner", "overdue" and "closed with evidence"?

## 2. Participants (small, formative)

| Group | Language | Number | Recruited from |
|---|---|---|---|
| Care coordinators / navigators | English | 3–5 | partner programme staff |
| GPs / specialists | English | 3 | partner clinics |
| Patients (screening age, mixed digital confidence) | English | 5 | community / patient panel |
| Patients | Arabic | 5 | community / patient panel, native readers |

Five per group finds most serious problems in formative testing. Staff UI is English only by design; patient UI is
tested in Arabic as well as English (Hindi and Urdu next round).

## 3. Setup

* Synthetic records only, on a test copy; demo accounts per role. Never real patient data.
* Phone (patients) and laptop (staff); screen and audio recording with written consent; no names in notes.
* Moderator (script below) plus a note-taker; Arabic sessions moderated by a native speaker.
* 30 minutes per participant; a shopping voucher if the partner's rules allow.

## 4. Tasks

**Staff**
1. "Start of shift: what needs you first?" (work queue).
2. "This critical result was phoned to you. Record it." (read-back form, then triage).
3. "This case is overdue. Who owns it and what is late?"
4. "Close this case — the specialist visit happened." (closed with evidence).
5. Admin only: "Is anything wrong with the system today?" (Admin → System, Monitoring).

**Patients (en, ar)**
1. Open the result message. "In your own words, what does it say and what do you do next?"
2. Answer the teach-back questions.
3. Confirm the appointment; then ask to change it.
4. "You do not want any more messages. Stop them." (How we may contact you.)
5. Arabic only: check layout reads right-to-left, numbers and dates are readable, nothing is cut off.

## 5. Measures

* Task success (unaided / with hint / failed), time on task, errors.
* Teach-back pass on first attempt (patients).
* Single Ease Question (1–7) after each task; System Usability Scale at the end (staff).
* Words or labels participants did not understand (verbatim, translated for Arabic).

## 6. Stop and escalate rules

* Any participant who believes a synthetic result is about them: stop, explain, offer the contact of a clinician.
* A patient task that fails for 3 of 5 participants in one language: fix before any pilot in that language.
* A staff task 2 or 3 that fails for 2 or more clinicians: fix before shadow mode (`docs/operating_modes.md`).
